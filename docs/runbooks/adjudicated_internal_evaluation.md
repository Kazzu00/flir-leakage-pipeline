## Population and isolation rule

candidate. Objective identity, decoding, annotation correspondence, YOLO geometry
and class-ID checks determine technical eligibility, before any detector output.
No record is selected according to difficulty, predictions or its effect on mAP.
An invalid anchor stays protected rather than entering development.

Verified equal image SHA256 defines the accepted exact-content units. No applicable
previously established automatic nonexact acceptance rule was found for the
available FAC candidates. Cosine scores, nominal timestamps and membership of
retrieval support groups do not qualify as acceptance.

A separate conservative protection graph uses already enumerated candidate edges,
including their out-of-population content endpoints. Components connected to the
historical protected envelope are excluded from development in both policies.

thresholds, support-group cliques, feature extraction or nearest-neighbor search
are introduced. Absence of recorded edges does not establish independence.

and development. Protected contents outside the test have a deterministic reserve
representative; duplicate occurrences remain quarantined. The executor explicitly
maps reserve and quarantine to its excluded `quarantine` value while preserving
